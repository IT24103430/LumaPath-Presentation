# LumaPath AI — presentation script

This script follows the seven scroll chapters. The on-screen presentation is an interactive, illustrative prototype.

## 01 — The vision

**On screen:** Your future has a next step.

**Voiceover:** Every student has a destination. The difficult part is knowing what to do next, right now. LumaPath turns that uncertainty into a clear, realistic path.

## 02 — The problem

**On screen:** One student. Too many moving parts.

**Voiceover:** Academic deadlines, career goals, skill gaps, and personal capacity often live in separate places. Students are left to connect everything themselves.

## 03 — The idea

**On screen:** One connected decision.

**Voiceover:** LumaPath brings those signals together. Rules identify priority and risk. Planning logic finds a realistic time. Clear explanations show the student why.

## 04 — The moment

**On screen:** The next best action, made obvious.

**Voiceover:** Instead of another overwhelming dashboard, the student sees one useful action, the reason it matters, and a manageable plan for today.

## 05 — The response

**On screen:** Life changes. The plan adapts.

**Voiceover:** When a deadline moves or available capacity falls, LumaPath protects urgent work and reschedules flexible learning. The plan changes with the student.

## 06 — The future

**On screen:** Progress you can see. Skills you can show.

**Voiceover:** The career roadmap links learning, practice, and project evidence. Students can see where they are and what to build next.

## 07 — The promise

**On screen:** Know what to do next.

**Voiceover:** Academic success and career growth belong in one plan. LumaPath helps students stay on track and build their future, one achievable step at a time.
